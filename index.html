About Unlock Potential

Meet Vimary

As the Founder of Unlock Potential, she partners with small and mid-sized businesses as well as enterprise teams to design and execute HR strategies that align talent with business outcomes. Her work sits at the intersection of HR consulting, talent strategy, and transformational coaching—helping organizations not only hire better, but lead more effectively and retain top talent.

With 20+ years of experience across consulting and diverse industries, she brings a practical, business-first approach to executive and leadership hiring, organizational transformation (including M&A and restructuring), talent strategy and workforce planning, as well as leadership development.

In parallel, she coaches leaders and transitioning military professionals who are seeking clarity, alignment, and meaningful next steps in their careers. Her coaching is grounded in her signature Unlock Potential Coaching Experience, powered by the R.E.A.C.H. framework—guiding individuals from clarity to aligned action and sustained growth.

Whether working with organizations or individuals, her focus is the same: unlocking potential so people can perform at their highest level and create lasting impact.
